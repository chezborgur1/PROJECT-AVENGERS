# Intake call: Ridgeline Comfort Services (FICTIONAL SAMPLE BUSINESS)

This is fictional sample data used to demonstrate the AMB material bot.

## Business
- Residential and light-commercial HVAC service, replacement and maintenance, plus plumbing service added in 2019.
- Founded 2009 by the current owner, Mark Delaney, after 12 years as a lead technician at a regional HVAC contractor.
- Headquartered in Columbus, Ohio. Serves Franklin, Delaware and Licking counties (about a 35-mile radius).
- Revenue mix FY2025: residential service & replacement ~64%, commercial ~27%, maintenance agreements ~9%.
- 1,450 active maintenance-agreement members (two tune-ups a year, priority scheduling, 15% repair discount). Renewal rate about 82%.
- Commercial customers: property managers, small office buildings, 4 restaurant groups. Largest single customer is about 4% of revenue.
- Google rating 4.8 from 900+ reviews. Carrier Factory Authorized Dealer since 2015.

## People
- 24 full-time employees: 14 service technicians, 4 install crew, 1 operations manager, 3 CSRs / dispatch, 1 bookkeeper, plus the owner.
- Operations manager (Lisa Tran) has been with the company 11 years, runs dispatch and day-to-day scheduling, and is expected to stay.
- Owner works about 45 hours/week: estimating large replacements, commercial relationships, vendor terms and hiring.
- Technicians are paid hourly plus spiff commissions on maintenance agreements. No union.

## Operations
- Leases an 8,000 sq ft shop and office on Maple Ave; lease runs through 2029 with a 5-year renewal option. Landlord is unrelated to the owner.
- Fleet of 18 service vans and 2 install trucks (owned, financed with vehicle loans).
- Runs on ServiceTitan (dispatch, invoicing, memberships) and QuickBooks Online.
- Main suppliers: Ferguson and Johnstone Supply; Carrier equipment through the local distributor.

## Transaction
- Reason for sale: owner retirement (age 61). Wants a clean exit within 12 months.
- Owner is willing to stay up to 12 months for transition and introduce commercial accounts.
- Prefers an asset sale; open to a buyer keeping the Ridgeline name and all employees.
- Market salary for a general manager to replace the owner: roughly $95,000.

## Growth opportunities (owner's view)
- Raise maintenance-agreement penetration: currently offered on ~35% of service calls.
- Add after-hours emergency plumbing (currently turned away after 7pm).
- Expand commercial preventive-maintenance contracts; two property managers have asked for quotes on additional buildings.
- Little paid digital marketing today; most leads are referrals and Google reviews.
- A second location in the Dayton market has been discussed but never pursued.
