## 1. Summary

1. **AMB produces two families of output that never meet on the same page.** Word memos/reports carry the argument (Times New Roman, maroon headings, crimson header band). Excel-exported PDF packs carry the numbers (Arial, maroon table headers, yellow boxed totals), with **no prose, no takeaways and no source lines at all**. The only place exhibits and commentary sit together is the Topkey Pre-LOI memo, where Excel screenshots are pasted under "Commentary:" paragraphs. A top-tier CIM page merges the two: an action title, an exhibit, and 2–4 quantified bullets beside it.
2. **The Opportunity Report v3 is the house voice at its best** and should be the stylistic template for all bot prose: an opinionated "Our view:" lead, H2s that state conclusions ("Concentration is above average"), numbers in half of all sentences, mechanism explained ("Because staff are paid a fixed amount, margin per client depends almost entirely on how many clients each person carries"), and explicit judgements with dollar impacts ("Using the active roster would overstate earnings by about $63K, or roughly $440K of value at a 7x multiple").
3. **Brand palette is consistent across both families:** crimson `#990033` (header band, cover bars, Word table headers, callout rule), maroon `#7B0000`/`#7A0000` (headings, exhibit table headers, single-series bars), pink `#FFE1E1` (section rows), light yellow `#FFFFCC` (boxed key totals), `#F7EBEF` callout fill, `#EADDE1` subtotal tint, `#F2F2F2` zebra. Off-palette leftovers: Excel green `#70AD47`, cyan `#00B0F0`, red `#FF0000`, Sunshine's blue `#DDEBF7` and grey `#D9D9D9` bars, `#FFF2CC` and `#F4CCCC` Word table headers.
4. **Adjusted EBITDA is always two-tier**: "Near-Term Adj. EBITDA" (changes made within the first year post-close) and "Steady-State Adj. EBITDA" (earnings excluding the growth engine), each built as a signed bridge with `(+)`/`(–)` labels. Main Street deals use an SDE bridge (Net Income → `(+) Interest` → `(+) Depreciation` → `(-) Capital Reserve` → Adj. EBITDA → `(+) Owner Compensation` → SDE → `(x) Valuation Multiple`).
5. **Summary blocks exist in three forms**: "Quick Stats (as of July 2026)" (bold label: value, italic margin, then footnotes `(1)…(3)`), "Key Stats" (two lines, Sunshine), and the Opportunity Report's two-column **"Key Metric | Finding"** table (10 rows, crimson header, zebra rows). The table form is the one to keep: it is dense, sourced in-line, and does not look like AI stat tiles.
6. **Risks are framed as a three-column table: "Risk | What We See | Potential Mitigant"**, 8 rows, every "What We See" cell quantified ("24 departures since May; resignations up from 7 in 2025 to 15 in 2026"). The earlier memo only lists three maroon stub headings.
7. **Diligence-to-value framing is a strength**: the "Priority P&L Considerations" table ("Consideration | Impact | Verification") and the "Payroll judgments we made" section attach a dollar impact and a verification path to every open item. This translates directly into a CIM "Adjustments and basis of preparation" page.
8. **Exhibit titling is descriptive, not conclusive**: "Client Retention Charts", "Monthly Expense Trends ($000s)", "Summary P&L Overview ($000s)". Units always in the subtitle. Chart titles name series and period ("Monthly STR Subscription Revenue (Jan'24 - July'26)"). No exhibit in the slice has a "Source:" line; notes are rare and ad hoc ("Note: Cells highligted in green clear the $1M CRE valuation threshhold").
9. **Density**: memos run 118–372 words/page; exhibit packs run 1.4–3.5 exhibits/page with zero words of interpretation. Retention tables drop to **5.9pt** Arial across 18 columns, which is below any bank standard.
10. **QA is the biggest weakness**: numbers drift between documents (Steady-State Adj. EBITDA is $449K in the Topkey memo and P&L Overview, $469K in the Pre-LOI pack, $551K in the Opportunity Report; Karbon LTM spend $79K vs $90K), copy-paste subtitle errors (employee charts titled "Client Retention Charts"; "Employee Hires & Offboarding Overview ($000s)" for a page of dates), a valuation table whose "Average Valuation" shows the rent total ($4,075), placeholders left in ("[ ]"), bare stubs, and typos ("VT Platform", "Inital", "Intitial", "Extimated", "highligted", "threshhold", "Rennovation").
11. **Standard CIM pages to build from AMB's library** (detail in §13): Key Metrics table, Investment Highlights, Historical P&L summary (FY/YTD/LTM/PF + delta), Adjusted EBITDA bridge (two-tier, footnoted), Revenue composition (stacked monthly mix + mix/growth table), Core revenue trajectory, Customer retention bridge + wins/losses chart, Customer concentration (Top 20, coded), Unit economics/staffing load, Team and tenure, Cost structure trends with % of revenue overlay, Technology stack, Real estate/market rent comps and cap-rate sensitivity (asset-heavy deals), Valuation sensitivity grid, Adjustments basis-of-preparation table.

## 2. Document types and their section structures

### 2.1 Pre-LOI Deal Memo (`AMB + Topkey: Pre-LOI Deal Memo.docx`)

Buy-side memo for the client (Topkey). Header band reads **"Keystone - Initial Financial Review"** (the file name says "Pre-LOI Deal Memo": naming is inconsistent). Six pages, US Letter portrait, 1in margins.

Section order, exact headings (all Times New Roman 16pt bold `#7B0000`):

1. **Quick Stats** *(as of July 2026)* (the parenthetical is bold italic)
2. **Executive Summary** (placeholder "[ ]": never written)
3. **Strategic Considerations**
   - Question sub-head, 12pt bold black: "What are the strategic rationales for acquiring Keystone?"
   - One framing sentence, then a numbered list with bold lead-ins: "Strengthen product package:", "Develop trust accounting:", "Keystone margin expansion:"
   - Then four bare bold bullets (unfinished): "Migrating Software to Topkey Products", "Implementing Software for Existing Clients", "Software Engineer Savings", "Topkey Client Cross-Sell"
4. **Deal Risks & Mitigants**: numbered maroon 12pt bold stubs only: "Leadership Turnover", "Staffing Turnover", "Customer Turnover"
5. **Priority P&L Considerations**: 3-column table (see §6.3)
6. **Revenue Analysis**: pasted "Revenue Summary" table image, "Commentary:", then two chart titles (12pt bold maroon) each followed by a chart image and a "Commentary:" paragraph
7. **Expense Analysis**: "Monthly Indirect Wages (Jan'24 - July'26)", "Monthly Bookkeeper Wages (Jan'24 - July'26)", "Monthly Onboarding Wages (Jan'24 - July'26)", each chart + "Commentary:"
8. **Software Overview**: "Top 10 Vendors" table image + "Commentary:", then "Monthly Software Expense Excluding Topkey (Jan'24 - July'26)" chart + "Commentary:"

Pattern worth keeping: **exhibit title (maroon) → exhibit → "Commentary:" paragraph (bold lead-in, 2–3 sentences, every sentence quantified).** In the render, LibreOffice pushes several chart titles to the bottom of a page and the chart to the next (pages 3–5): the chart titles are not set to keep-with-next. Charts are 6.5in wide × 2.2–2.6in tall PNG screenshots (1,219–1,291 px wide), so they are soft and use Arial while the body uses Times.

## 3. Visual system

### 3.1 Word memos and reports

- **Header band**: full-bleed PNG, solid `#990033`, 8.54in × 0.79in (Topkey memo; `wp:extent 7810500 × 719138` EMU) anchored at page top, behind text. Title centred in Times New Roman 15–16pt bold white; white knockout AMB logo (2048 × 698 px source) at right.
- **Footer**: grey `#A0A0A0` hairline + centred page number (Arial 10pt).
- **Headings**: Times New Roman bold `#7B0000`; H1 16–17pt (v3: 15pt before / 6pt after), H2 12.5pt (10pt before / 4pt after), exhibit titles 12pt. keepNext is set on H1/H2 in the v3 report only; the Topkey memo's chart titles lack it.
- **Body**: Times New Roman 11.5–12pt black. Lists: bullets "•" or numerals in bold maroon (Next Steps).
- **Tables (v3)**: 6.5in wide (9,360 dxa); header row `#990033` fill, white bold 10pt; body 10pt; zebra `#FFFFFF`/`#F2F2F2`; bold subtotal rows on `#EADDE1`; 0.5pt `#BFBFBF` borders; cell padding 3pt top/bottom, 5pt sides; numeric columns right-aligned; header row repeats and rows can't split. Column grids: Key Metric 3,700 / Finding 5,660 dxa; bridges 6,360 / 3,000; risks 2,600 / 3,380 / 3,380; P&L summary 3,360 / 2,000 × 3.
- **Tables (Topkey memo)**: 1pt black grid, header `#FFF2CC`, all cells centred (numbers not aligned: weaker).
- **Callouts (v3)**: paragraph with a 3pt `#990033` left border, `#F7EBEF` fill, 0.125in indent, bold maroon lead-in then black text. Three uses: "Takeaway:", "Opportunity:", "Long-term view:". This is AMB's only "highlight box", and it is restrained enough not to read as AI decoration.
- **Footnotes/notes**: 10pt; "(1) … (2) … (3) …" under Quick Stats; italic "Note: …" or explanatory sentence under tables; italic disclaimer "This structure should be confirmed with a qualified tax advisor. AMB is not providing tax or legal advice."

### 3.2 PDF exhibit packs

## 6. How AMB explains adjusted EBITDA and add-backs

### 6.1 Two tiers, defined once

- **Near-Term Adj. EBITDA**: "accounts for personnel & software changes made within the first year post-close". Contains definitional adjustments, personal expenses, one-off professional fees, the new hire the buyer must make, go-forward owner comp, and software savings the buyer controls.
- **Steady-State Adj. EBITDA**: "represents the earnings profile excluding the company's growth engine costs". Removes excess staffing, growth-stage onboarding and S&M spend, and further vendor replacement.

### 6.2 The bridges (v3 report, verbatim labels, PF Jul'26, $000s)

Near-Term Bridge: Pro forma net income $51 · (+) Definitional EBITDA and personal expense adjustments 19 · (–) Onboarding manager hire (116) · (–) Go-forward owner compensation (120) · **Pro forma earnings before savings ($166)** · (+) Karbon, Uncat, and Xenett savings 97 · (+) R&D add-back (Other Professional Fees) 73 · (+) Unclassified active client contribution 51 · **Near-term adjusted EBITDA $54**.

Steady-State Bridge: Near-term adjusted EBITDA $54 · (+) Normalized bookkeeper staffing 220 · (+) VR Platform cost savings 179 · (+) Onboarding team wages 148 · (–) Onboarding revenue (78) · (+) S&M wages 28 · **Steady-state adjusted EBITDA $551** · % Margin 17.8%.

Good habits in how the bridge is narrated:
- An intermediate subtotal ("Pro forma earnings before savings") isolates the part of the bridge that depends on the buyer executing.
- Each adjustment is sized against its LTM evidence: "The VR Platform adjustment ($179K) reflects run-rate spend, which is above the $161K LTM figure."
- A downside case is given in the same paragraph: "A more conservative assumption that removes only 25% of net onboarding cost would reduce steady-state EBITDA by about $47K, to roughly $504K."
- The key driver is named and bounded: "The valuation hinges on the steady-state bookkeeper load… We view a load of 8 as a conservative, achievable target."
- What is **not** assumed is stated: "Our pro forma does not assume Topkey replaces property management systems or Bill Pay, nor that Topkey is sold into clients not already using it. Each is incremental upside."

## 10. Writing voice

### 10.1 Characteristics (measured)

- **Sentence length**: median 16.5 words (v3 report, 208 sentences), 13 words (Topkey memo), 14 (Sunshine memo). Short declaratives mixed with one longer explanatory sentence per paragraph.
- **Numbers per sentence**: 50% of v3 sentences contain a figure; 59% in the Topkey memo; 67% in the Sunshine memo.
- **Stance**: first-person plural advisor ("Our view:", "we believe", "We recommend", "We would like to confirm", "our steady-state case removes"). Judgements are explicit ("which makes $3.0M attractive", "is a genuine strength", "is the most concerning element of the data").
- **Money style in prose**: $2.55M, $998K, ~$54K, –$166K (minus sign), ranges with en dash ($500K–$700K, 8–10 clients); hedges "~", "roughly", "about", "approximately". Tables use $000s with parentheses.
- **Period shorthand**: LTM, PF, run rate, Y/Y, m/m, FY2024, "July'26", "Jan'24".
- **Structure words**: "Takeaway:", "Opportunity:", "Long-term view:", "Commentary:", "Note:", "Our view:"; bold lead-in on nearly every bullet.
- **Mechanism before verdict**: comparisons "from X to Y", ratios ("roughly 1.7x that of non-Bill Pay clients"), then the implication.
- **Uncertainty is flagged, not hidden**: "Initial", "pending verification", "may be understated", "should be reconciled with Keystone".
- **No marketing adjectives.** "Well-positioned", "robust", "best-in-class" do not occur in the slice. The strongest adjectives are "attractive", "genuine", "materially", "meaningfully", "unusually strong".

### 10.3 Footnote and note conventions

- Numbered, parenthesised, no superscript, 10pt, directly under the block: "(1) Initial earnings figures are pending verification via Seller & Client discussion sessions".
- Asterisk on an unverified label, italic explanation under the table: "Turnaround Testron*" → "*Cannot verify through Google Maps -- appears to be Carrie's Beauty Salon".
- "Note:" sentence under an exhibit, italic 10pt in Word: "Note: the software data covers 174 current clients, while the retention analysis shows 190 active clients; the difference should be reconciled with Keystone."
- Assumption line under grids: "Assumes a 20-year amortization schedule with 6.50% interest rate".
- Disclaimer, italic 10pt: "This structure should be confirmed with a qualified tax advisor. AMB is not providing tax or legal advice."

**Bot rule:** keep "(1) pending verification"-style footnotes, but attach the marker to the figure (e.g., "$449K⁽¹⁾" or "$449K (1)"), and use them for every unverified or management-provided number. That also satisfies the CLAUDE.md rule that unknowns become `[Confirm: …]` rather than invented facts.

## 12. What is weak and should be elevated

**Structure and argument**
1. Exhibit packs are databooks, not documents: 50 exhibits in 31 pages, no contents page, no commentary, no ordering by argument, repeated pages (P&L tables reappear at p29–31). CIM: order pages by the investment thesis and give each an action title.
2. Titles describe rather than conclude ("Client Retention Charts"). Replace with sentence headlines, e.g., "Client wins exceeded losses in 17 of the last 21 months; May-26 (7 wins, 15 losses) was the outlier" (counts from Pre-LOI p7).
3. Word memos contain unfinished sections ("Executive Summary [ ]", bare bullets under Strategic Considerations, risk stubs, a yellow-highlighted to-do). The bot must never ship placeholders other than `[Confirm: …]`.

**Numbers and QA**

## 14. Recommended CIM page grammar (AMB conventions, executed at bank level)

- **Every content page**: action title (one sentence, 16–18pt bold `#7B0000`) → thin rule → exhibit(s) → 2–4 commentary bullets (bold lead-in + number) → source line (8pt grey) → footnotes → page number and "Confidential" footer. Units in the exhibit subtitle, AMB-style "($000s)".
- **Fonts**: Arial for all tables and charts (AMB exhibit convention); prose either Times New Roman (AMB memo continuity) or a cleaner serif; never mix inside an exhibit.
- **Tables**: AMB two-tier maroon header, pink section rows, boxed yellow key totals, right-aligned numbers, `$` first/total rows only, parentheses negatives, en dash zeros, 8pt minimum.
- **Charts**: native, maroon anchor + tonal ramp + greys, no outlines, no 3-D, no rotated labels, one annotation that supports the headline.
- **Emphasis**: at most one `#F7EBEF` left-rule callout per page ("Takeaway:"), no cards, no circles, no numbered badges, no stat tiles, no letter-spaced kickers.
- **Voice**: v3 report. Lead with the conclusion, give the mechanism, quantify, flag what is unverified. Ban list for prose: "well-positioned", "robust", "best-in-class", "poised", "leverage synergies", "seamless", "cutting-edge", "unlock value".
